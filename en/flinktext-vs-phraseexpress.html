Flinktext or PhraseExpress?

As of 13 November 2026. Details on PhraseExpress come from its maker's website and PC-WELT (sources below).

Both insert snippets into any program when you type an abbreviation. PhraseExpress can do a lot more. Flinktext does less and is deliberately kept simple. Here is an honest comparison.

PhraseExpress fits if you …

need macros, a clipboard history or team features, also work on a Mac or iPhone, or want to use it for free for purely personal use.

Flinktext fits if you …

want to start quickly with few settings, no account and no cloud, and prefer one small one-time payment for work use too.

Cost

PhraseExpress is free for personal users. According to its maker, anyone who uses it for work that earns money needs a licence after 30 days. Flinktext is one small one-time purchase for everyone, for personal and work use alike, after a free 30-day trial.

Switching from PhraseExpress

Flinktext has no direct import from PhraseExpress. A few snippets are quickest to move by copy and paste. For many, create a table with the columns abbreviation, text and name, save it as CSV and import it into Flinktext (guide). Then check any PhraseExpress placeholders and replace them with Flinktext's own (list).
