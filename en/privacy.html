Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026 · covers the Windows app Flinktext and this website. The German version is the binding one.

In short

  • Flinktext stores your snippets only on your PC.
  • The app makes no internet connections. There is no account, no cloud, no advertising, no usage statistics.

2. The Flinktext app

Data on your PC

Flinktext stores your snippets, settings and automatic backups in a folder of your Windows user account, plus a log file with technical messages, for example whether the start worked. The log contains nothing from your snippets. This data does not leave your PC. We as the provider have no access to it. When you uninstall the app, Windows deletes this folder.

Keystrokes

When you switch on abbreviation detection, Flinktext watches which keys you press in order to recognise your abbreviations. It keeps only the last few characters in memory and compares them with your abbreviations. These characters are not stored, not logged and not transmitted. They are discarded when you switch to another window or click the mouse. You can switch abbreviation detection off in the settings at any time; the quick search and hotkeys keep working.

Clipboard

To insert a text, Flinktext places it in the clipboard briefly and then puts the previous content back. Inserted snippets are marked so that Windows keeps them out of the clipboard history and out of syncing between devices. With the placeholder {clipboard}, the app reads the current clipboard content, only at the moment you insert that snippet.

No internet connection

Flinktext sends no data to us or to anyone else. The app's windows talk to each other over a connection that exists only inside your PC (address 127.0.0.1) and is protected by a secret key. Flinktext shows its windows with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows. Whether this component sends diagnostic data to Microsoft depends on your Windows settings under Privacy & security.

3. Microsoft Store

Flinktext is offered through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles purchase, payment, the trial period, licence checks and updates. Microsoft is responsible for this data; the Microsoft Privacy Statement applies. As the developer we only receive aggregated reports from Microsoft, for example the number of purchases and installs, ratings and crash reports from Windows Error Reporting. They do not tell us who you are or what your snippets contain.

5. If you write to us

If you email us, we use your address and your message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them. Please do not send us confidential texts from your snippets.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). An email to the address above is enough. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

Since the app itself sends no data to us, we hold no data about you from it. You delete your snippets in the app or by uninstalling it.

7. Changes

If something about the app changes that affects privacy, we update this policy. The version published here applies.
