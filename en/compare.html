Text expanders for Windows compared

As of 13 November 2026. Details on other programs come from their makers' websites (sources below). Prices and features can change.

There are many good text expanders. Here you can see how they differ, when Flinktext is a good fit, and when another program is the better choice.

Flinktext is a good fit if …

  • you want a simple window instead of text files or scripts,
  • your text should stay on your PC: no account, no cloud, no data collection,
  • you would rather pay once than subscribe, for personal and work use alike,
  • you need your snippets in every program, not just Outlook.

Another program fits better if …

  • it must be free and text files or scripts don't bother you: Espanso or AutoHotkey.
  • you need macros, a clipboard history or team features: PhraseExpress.
  • you also work on a Mac or iPhone, or want your snippets synced between devices: TextExpander or PhraseExpress.
  • you only need a few snippets in Outlook: the parts built into Outlook are enough.

What Flinktext deliberately does not do

  • No sync between PCs. You move with a file ("Save to file" and "Load from file").
  • Windows only, no Mac and no phone.
  • No macros and no control of other programs. Flinktext inserts text, nothing more.
  • No pictures in snippets. Bold, italic and lists work.

Switch in a minute

Flinktext takes over your snippets and abbreviations directly from Beeftext, Espanso and AutoHotkey, and from CSV exports of TextExpander and aText. It converts placeholders such as date, clipboard and cursor. See Help, "Move in" for the steps.
