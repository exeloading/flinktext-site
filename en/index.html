How it works

  1. Enter your text
    A sign-off, a reply, an address, whatever you type often. Plus an abbreviation like /br.
  2. Type the abbreviation
    In any program: Outlook, Word, the browser, chat, your business software.
  3. Done
    Flinktext puts the text in. Typo? Backspace takes it back right away.

Quick search over any program

Can't remember the abbreviation? Ctrl + Shift + Space opens the search. Type two letters, press Enter. Important texts can get a hotkey of their own.

Small forms

Flinktext asks for a name, a date or a choice while inserting and puts everything in the right place. Plus placeholders for today's date, "in 14 days", the time, the weekday and where the cursor should end up.
