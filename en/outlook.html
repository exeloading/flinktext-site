Text snippets in Outlook

As of 13 November 2026.

Sign-offs, answers to common questions, appointment confirmations: with Flinktext you type an abbreviation in Outlook and the whole text is there. The same snippets work in your browser, in Word and in chat too.

Doesn't Outlook have its own?

It does. Outlook has Quick Parts, and the new Outlook also has "My Templates". For a few texts you only use in Outlook, that is enough. Flinktext is worth it if you

  • would rather type an abbreviation than click through menus,
  • need the same texts outside Outlook too, for example in the browser or in Teams,
  • want questions while inserting, for example for a name and a date,
  • want dates like "in 14 days" filled in automatically.

In three steps

  1. In Flinktext, click + on the left. Name "Appointment", abbreviation /appt, and the text below.
  2. Under "Insert mode", choose "Insert formatted" so bold text arrives in bold.
  3. In Outlook, open a new email and type /appt. Flinktext asks for the greeting, name, date and time and fills everything in.
{choice:Greeting|Hi|Hello} {input:Name},

thanks for getting in touch. Your appointment is booked:
**{datefield:Date|today+7} at {input:Time|2 pm}**

{cursor}

Best regards

After inserting, the cursor sits where {cursor} was, so you can keep typing.

More ideas for email

  • Sign-off under every email: /br.
  • Follow-up: "I'll get back to you by {date+14}."
  • Common questions such as opening hours, prices or directions, each with its own abbreviation.
  • Call note with {date}, {time} and {input:Caller}.

Only active in Outlook

To make a snippet work only in Outlook, enter the program file under "Only in this program":

OutlookEntry
classic Outlookoutlook.exe
new Outlookolk.exe
bothoutlook.exe, olk.exe
Outlook in the browserthe browser, for example msedge.exe or chrome.exe (then it applies in the whole browser)

If something doesn't work

The old clipboard content is pasted

Under Settings, "Advanced", raise the wait before restoring, for example to 800. More in the Help.

Bold and lists are missing

Set the snippet's insert mode to "Insert formatted". If the email is set to plain text, only plain text arrives.

The abbreviation is not replaced

Check whether Flinktext is paused and whether "Only in this program" names the right Outlook (see the table above).
