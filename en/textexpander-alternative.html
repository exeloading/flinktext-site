A TextExpander alternative without a subscription

As of 13 November 2026. Details on TextExpander come from its maker's website (source below).

TextExpander is strong, especially for teams and on Mac and iPhone. But it needs a subscription and an account. If you only work on Windows, Flinktext is a simple alternative: pay once, no account, and your text stays on your PC.

Switch in three steps

  1. In TextExpander, export the group with your snippets as CSV. Export several groups one by one.
  2. In Flinktext, open the settings, go to "Move in", click "Take over from another app" and choose the files.
  3. Look at the report. Flinktext converts date, clipboard, cursor, Tab and Enter, nested snippets and fill-in fields into its own placeholders. Anything it can't translate safely is listed under "Please check".

The snippets you already have in Flinktext stay, the new ones are added.

What TextExpander does on top

  • Share and manage snippets centrally in a team.
  • Mac, iPhone and a browser extension.
  • Sync between several devices.

If you need that, TextExpander is the better choice. If you don't, Flinktext lets you pay once instead of subscribing.
